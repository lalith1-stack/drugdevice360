Why programmes stall

The drug is ready. The device often isn't.

Most combination product delays come from the gaps between teams, not from the science. These are the four we see most.

01

Drug and device teams work in silos

Formulation, CMC and device engineering each make decisions in isolation, and the conflicts surface at integration.

Our approachOne team reviews viscosity, fill volume and injection time together, before the platform is locked.

02

Human factors arrives too late

Use errors found in a summative study mean redesign, new test data and a delayed filing.

Our approachURRA and formative studies start at the concept stage, while design changes are still cheap.

03

Design verification gets repeated

Vague design inputs and unvalidated test methods lead to failed or repeated DV runs.

Our approachMeasurable, traceable requirements and validated methods before the first DV protocol is signed.

04

Regulatory paths are unclear

PMOA, 21 CFR Part 4 and Article 117 Notified Body Opinions each bring their own documentation expectations.

Our approachA US and EU route map agreed at the start, so the DHF is built once for both.

FAQ

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly on a discovery call.

Do you run laboratory testing yourselves?

We design the test strategy, write protocols, develop and validate methods, and interpret the data. Physical testing runs at qualified partner laboratories, which we name in your proposal and coordinate on your behalf.

Can you work alongside our CDMO or device supplier?

Yes. Much of our work sits between the sponsor, the fill-finish CDMO and the device platform supplier. We act as your technical representative so requirements, risks and test data line up across all three.

Which markets do you support?

Our focus is the United States (FDA, 21 CFR Part 4) and the European Union (EU MDR, Article 117). We also support the UK, Canada, Australia, the Middle East, Latin America and India.

We are a small biotech. Is this affordable?

Our lean, senior model is built for exactly that. Most clients start with a fixed-fee gap assessment, then add workstreams once they've seen the quality of our output.

How do you protect our confidential information?

We sign your NDA or ours before any technical discussion, limit access to the named project team and return or destroy materials at the end of the engagement.
